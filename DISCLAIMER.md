# Disclaimer

Poker Finance is an educational game made by DMU Quant Society, a student society at De Montfort University, Leicester.

## Not affiliated

The game names real banks, trading firms and hedge funds only to describe their publicly reported styles of business. **No firm is affiliated with, sponsors or endorses this game**, and neither De Montfort University nor De Montfort Students' Union is responsible for its content.

## Not real strategies

Every bot is a caricature built from general public descriptions (for example, "market makers earn the bid–ask spread"). The bots do not reproduce, and do not claim to reproduce, any firm's actual trading strategies, models or risk limits.

## No logos or trademarks

The game uses firm names as plain text only. It does not use logos, brand colours or other trademarked imagery.

## Play money only

Chips have no value. Nothing can be bought, wagered, won or cashed out. There are no prizes.

## Not financial advice

Nothing in the game or its desk notes is financial or investment advice.

## Removing real names

If any firm prefers not to be named, contact the society and we will switch that firm (or all firms) to fictional aliases. All aliases already exist in `src/bots/firms.ts`, and `USE_REAL_NAMES` in `src/config.ts` switches every name at once.
