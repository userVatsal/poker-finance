# Code of Conduct

Poker Finance is a DMU Quant Society project. We want it to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background, course, experience or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone starts somewhere.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Sharing anyone's private information without permission.
- Anything that would breach De Montfort Students' Union or university conduct rules.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in any society space where the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the society committee through the DMU Quant Society LinkedIn page or the Students' Union. Reports are handled confidentially. The committee may remove content, block contributors or escalate to the Students' Union where appropriate.
