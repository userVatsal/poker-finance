# Privacy notice

Poker Finance is run by DMU Quant Society, a student society at De Montfort University, Leicester. This notice explains what data the game collects and why.

## The short version

- No accounts, no sign-up, no payments, no ads.
- Your display name stays in your own browser and is never sent to us.
- We count visits anonymously with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
- We use Google Analytics **only if you click "Accept analytics"**. If you decline, Google receives nothing.

## What we collect

| What                                                                                                                                        | Tool                          | Cookies              | When                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Anonymous page-view counts, referring site, country, device type                                                                            | Vercel Web Analytics          | None                 | Always (no personal data, no cross-site tracking) |
| How the game is used: games started, hands played, cash-outs, shares, which firm profiles are opened, plus Google's standard page-view data | Google Analytics 4            | Yes (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) | Only after you accept                             |
| Your optional display name and cookie choice                                                                                                | Your browser (`localStorage`) | No                   | Stored on your device only                        |

Fonts are served from our own site, so nothing is requested from Google unless you accept analytics. We do not collect your name, email or anything you type into the game. Google Analytics is set to anonymise IP addresses, and advertising features (ad storage, ad personalisation) are switched off.

## Why

To understand how people use the game so we can improve it, and to report reach to the society and its partners. Our lawful basis for Google Analytics is your consent.

## Your choices

- Change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer. Declining after accepting stops collection and deletes the Google Analytics cookies on this site.
- Clearing your browser's site data resets everything.
- Ad blockers and "Do Not Track" style tools may also block analytics. The game works the same either way.

## Who processes the data

- **Vercel Inc.** hosts the site and provides Web Analytics.
- **Google LLC** provides Google Analytics 4 (only with your consent). Data may be processed outside the UK under Google's standard safeguards.

Google Analytics data is kept for the shortest retention period available in the GA4 property settings (2 months by default for event data).

## Contact

Questions or requests about your data: message DMU Quant Society on LinkedIn, or open an issue at https://github.com/userVatsal/poker-finance.
